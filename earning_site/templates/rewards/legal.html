Important

Earnings are rewards for valid platform activity, not guaranteed income.

InvestHub can approve, reject, limit, hold, or reverse rewards if activity breaks rules, looks automated, abuses ads/offerwalls, or violates payment/ad-network compliance.

Create Account

One Real Account

Use one account per person/device unless admin approval is given. Duplicate accounts can be suspended and payouts can be blocked.

Complete Tasks Honestly

Timed tasks, proof fields, daily limits, and review status must be respected. Submitting fake proof or skipping timers may trigger fraud signals.

Withdrawals Need Review

Withdrawals require correct account details, OTP flow, and admin review. Pending requests are not final approved payments.

Allowed Earning Activities

  • Daily login bonus and streak rewards.
  • Valid ad/task completion with required timer.
  • Offerwall, survey, and app install tasks when verified.
  • Referral commissions from real invited users.
  • Spin rewards and membership bonuses within platform limits.

Not Allowed

  • Fake clicks, bots, scripts, auto-refresh, or click farms.
  • VPN/proxy abuse or hiding location/device identity.
  • Multiple accounts for the same person, device, or payout account.
  • Fake screenshots, false app install proof, or copied survey proof.
  • Asking others to click ads in a way that violates ad-network rules.

Fair Use Note

Rules can change when payment gateways, advertiser requirements, ad networks, offerwall providers, or anti-fraud policies change. The goal is to keep the platform safe, transparent, and usable for genuine users.
